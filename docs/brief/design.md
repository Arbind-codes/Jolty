# Jolty — Design brief

## Brand and experience

Create a premium, calm, mobile-first roadside assistance experience inspired by the clarity and confidence of leading on-demand services. Uber is a reference for simplicity and service confidence only; do not copy its interface, branding, wording, icons, or layouts.

The visual identity should feel useful and reassuring under stress, with a distinct Indian roadside character. Use subtle Indian roadside or truck-art cues as restrained accents, patterns, or illustration details. Avoid turning the entire interface into a novelty motif.

## Palette and imagery

- Cream / warm off-white for primary light surfaces.
- Black / deep charcoal for text and high-contrast structure.
- Saffron yellow as the signature action/accent color.
- Add neutrals and semantic colors only as needed for readable states; check contrast.
- Use a mix of daylight imagery and selected night imagery. The 2 AM breakdown is an important hero/story moment, but the whole product must not feel night-only. Dark imagery may be used in an intentional dark mode if one is implemented.
- Use original or properly licensed imagery. Do not use the referenced conversation's unseen images as a specification; their contents were not available in the project brief.

## Page guidance

### Landing
Lead with the benefit and a direct request-help action. Communicate that help can be requested day or night, with the late-night problem clearly featured. A visual showing a breakdown at 2 AM can be balanced with daylight service imagery. Keep location or booking entry prominent without overwhelming the hero.

### Login
Keep it uncluttered: one clear purpose, a short explanation, the chosen identity method, a primary action, and only necessary legal/help links. Avoid adding decorative blocks or multiple competing sign-in paths until authentication is decided.

### Mechanics list
Show provider identity and only trustworthy decision information: service fit, availability, distance/ETA where reliable, and price/quote status. Make selection easy to compare on a phone. Include loading, no-results, and error states.

### Order / checkout
Summarize location, vehicle/problem, selected provider, price and what it includes, payment timing, and relevant cancellation terms before the final action. Do not hide uncertainty in estimates.

### Live tracking
Show the order state, mechanic identity/contact route as approved, last update, and clear next step. Map movement must reflect actual updates; if live data is unavailable, use status updates rather than simulated precision.

## Responsive and accessibility behavior

- Design for narrow phone widths first, then tablet and desktop.
- Keep primary actions within comfortable thumb reach where possible.
- Use clear hierarchy, readable type, visible focus, semantic controls, sufficient contrast, and labels that do not rely on color alone.
- Keep essential instructions available in low-bandwidth or delayed-loading states.
